{# ── How to do each exercise ─────────────────────────────────────────────── #}
How to do each exercise
Tap a demo to pause it.
Warm up (8 min)
5 min brisk walk or marching, then 10 bodyweight squats, 10 hip hinges and 10 arm circles each way. Before the first lift: empty bar × 8, about half your work weight × 5, then about three quarters × 3.
Choose and add weight
Weeks 1–2: practise the movements with light weights. Then use a weight where you could do 2 more reps. When you finish every set at the target reps, add the "add when ready" amount next time.
Brace on every rep
Breathe into your belly and tighten your stomach as if you are about to be punched. Keep your back flat and the bar close. Stop if you feel sharp pain, as opposed to muscle burn.
Small plates?
Picking the bar up from the floor assumes full-size 45 cm plates. With smaller ones, raise the bar on blocks to mid-shin.
