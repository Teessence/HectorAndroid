Barbell Full-Body Plan

One full-body workout, the same six exercises every Monday, Wednesday and Friday. Repeating it means the movements become automatic quickly and progress is easy to track. Every lift starts from the floor, so all you need is the bar and plates. It is built to keep your muscle while you lose fat on a calorie deficit and 20,000 steps a day.

How every session runs

Warm up (8 min)

  • 5 min brisk walk or marching on the spot.
  • 10 bodyweight squats, 10 hip hinges, 10 arm circles each way.
  • Before the first lift: empty bar × 8, then about half your work weight × 5, then about three quarters × 3.

Choose the weight

  • Weeks 1–2: practise the movement with light weights.
  • Then use a weight where you could do 2 more reps at the end of each set.
  • Rest 2–3 min after big lifts and 1–2 min after the smaller ones.

Add weight

  • When every set reaches the top of the rep range, add weight next time: +2.5 kg for upper-body lifts, +5 kg for lower-body lifts.
  • Then build back up from the bottom of the range.
  • On a deficit progress is slower. Keeping the same weights still counts as a win.

Brace on every rep

  • Take a big breath into your belly and tighten your stomach as if you are about to be punched.
  • Keep your back flat and the bar close to your body.
  • Stop if you feel sharp pain, as opposed to muscle burn.

Getting the bar to your shoulders

Without a rack, the front squat and the overhead press both start with this move. Use it every time you need the bar on your shoulders.

Fitting it around fat loss

Protein

Eat about 1.6–2.2 g of protein per kg of your target weight every day, including rest days. Track it in Hector.

Steps

Keep walking. If your legs feel wrecked the day after a session, walk a little less that day instead of skipping the session.

Sleep

Aim for 7 hours or more. Most of your recovery, and most of the muscle you keep, depends on it.

The scale

The first 2–3 weeks of lifting add some water weight. Measure your waist every 2 weeks to see the real trend.

Small plates?

Picking the bar up from the floor (for the clean, the row and the Romanian deadlift) assumes full-size 45 cm plates, which put the bar at mid-shin. With smaller plates, raise the bar on books or blocks to that height so your back can stay flat.
